# Mission Reflection

Completing Mission 3 helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its strong capabilities in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I found its infrastructure useful for organizations that need modern technologies for applications and research.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, identity and access management, databases, security services, and tools for deploying applications. They also provide scalable infrastructure that allows organizations to increase or decrease resources according to their workload requirements.

Business requirements have an important influence on cloud platform selection. A startup with a limited budget may prioritize scalability and a broad selection of services. An organization that already depends heavily on Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an AI research organization may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI and machine-learning capabilities. This shows that there is no single cloud provider that is automatically the best for every organization.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to consider the relationship between technical capabilities and business requirements. Instead of selecting a provider based only on its popularity, I learned to examine the organization's existing technology, workload, budget, scalability requirements, and future goals.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added organized Markdown documentation, cloud platform comparisons, client recommendations, a decision matrix, and screenshots as evidence. This made my portfolio more professional and helped me practice documenting technical research in a structured way.
